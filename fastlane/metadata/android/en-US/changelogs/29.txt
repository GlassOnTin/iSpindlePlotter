Added a drag-select for marking bad data, and spunding guidance on the
fermentation stage card.

Mark bad data: press and drag along the graph to select a time range
— for readings recorded while the spindle sat out of the wort, or
across a WiFi gap — and mark it. Marked spans show as a shaded band
on the chart and are listed under the Graph screen; they stop shaping
the plots, the stage detection and the model fits. The recorded data
itself is never deleted: excluded readings stay in the database and
the CSV export, and a mark can be removed at any time.

Spunding guidance: the fermentation stage description now says when
it's safe to set ~10 psi of spunding pressure (once the ferment is
strong), and when to raise to ~15 psi (near final gravity, or as the
cold crash begins), with the target SG printed for this brew. Early
pressure slows yeast growth, so the card asks you to hold off while
the ferment is young.
